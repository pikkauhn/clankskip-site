ClankSkip

Skip AI music on Android

ClankSkip watches what's playing in your music app and skips songs by known AI-generated artists as soon as they start. It works with most music and streaming apps.

Coming soon

Android 8.0 or newer. Free, no ads, no accounts.

What it does

  • Skips AI artists automaticallyIn whatever music app you use, no setup per app.
  • Keeps a skip historySo you can see what was skipped and find out if a song you liked was AI.
  • Lets you report artistsMark an artist as AI and send them to the community list for review.
  • Handles mistakesTap "Not AI" on anything skipped by mistake and it won't be skipped again.

Where the list comes from

ClankSkip uses the community list of AI artists from spot-the-ai.com, which also builds on work by Soul Over AI. The list is downloaded every few hours and songs are checked on your phone, so nothing about what you listen to is sent anywhere.

Privacy

Your listening history stays on your phone. Reporting an artist sends only the artist's name and a random ID, and can be turned off. Read the full privacy policy.
